# 💰 Passive Income Opportunity Finder & Tracker

An interactive terminal application that helps you discover, analyze, and track various passive income opportunities including dividend stocks, REITs, bonds, high-yield savings accounts, and alternative investments.

## Features

### 🔍 Discovery & Analysis
- **16+ curated passive income opportunities** with real-world yields
- Browse by category: Dividend Stocks, REITs, ETFs, Bonds, Savings, Crypto, Alternative Investments
- Filter by risk level: Very Low to Very High
- Find low-entry opportunities (under $500 minimum investment)
- View top-yielding opportunities ranked by annual yield

### 📊 Portfolio Tools
- **Portfolio Income Calculator**: Input your investments and see projected monthly/annual income
- **Sample Diversified Portfolio**: View a balanced $50k portfolio example
- **Income Goal Calculator**: Determine how much to invest to reach your target passive income
- **10-Year Projections**: See compound growth with dividend reinvestment

## Risk Levels Explained

- **Very Low**: FDIC insured savings and CDs
- **Low**: Diversified dividend ETFs, Treasury bonds
- **Medium**: Blue-chip dividend stocks, quality REITs
- **High**: Mortgage REITs, P2P lending, crypto staking
- **Very High**: Volatile assets with regulatory or platform risk

## Pro Tips

1. **Diversify across categories** - Don't put all eggs in one basket
2. **Reinvest dividends** - Compound growth accelerates returns
3. **Match risk to timeline** - Higher risk acceptable for longer timeframes
4. **Start small** - Many opportunities have <$100 minimums
5. **Automate** - Set up automatic dividend reinvestment (DRIP)
6. **Tax efficiency** - Consider tax-advantaged accounts (IRA, 401k)
7. **Rebalance annually** - Maintain target allocation
